Privacy Policy — Feed Filter

Last updated: 29 July 2026 · Applies to Feed Filter version 0.4.0 and later

Feed Filter collects nothing. It has no server, makes no network requests of any kind, and contains no analytics, tracking or telemetry. Everything it records stays in your browser's local storage on your own device, and nobody — including the developer — can see it.

What the extension does

Feed Filter identifies which posts in your Facebook home feed come from friends you added and groups you joined, and lets you count, dim, hide or opt out of the rest. To do that it reads the content of Facebook pages you have opened yourself, in your browser, as they are displayed.

What is stored, and where

All of the following is written to your browser's local extension storage (storage.local). It never leaves your device. It is not synced between your browsers or computers.

WhatWhy
Your friends list — profile identifiers and display names, read from your own “All friends” page To recognise which posts are from people you added
Your groups — group identifiers and names, read from your own “Your groups” page To recognise which posts are from groups you joined
Daily counts — how many posts fell into each category, per day, kept for 30 days To show what your feed is made of
A log of sources you hid — the name of each page, group or person you told Facebook to stop showing you, and when Facebook keeps no such list; this is the only record you have of it
Your settings To remember your choices
Diagnostics — up to 20 recent entries recording which detection strategy matched. These contain no names, no identifiers and no post content whatsoever, only true/false values and strategy names. To make it possible to fix the extension when Facebook changes its website

The content of posts is never stored

Post text is read into memory in order to classify a post and to recognise the same post appearing twice, and is then discarded. It is never written to storage, never retained after the page is closed, and never transmitted anywhere.

What the extension never does

  • It makes no network requests. There is no server to send anything to.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry of any kind.
  • It does not sell, share or transmit your data to anyone, for any purpose.
  • It does not use cookies.
  • It does not read pages on any website other than Facebook.
  • It does not read your Facebook password, messages, or account credentials.
  • It does not scroll, browse or navigate on your behalf to gather information.

Permissions, and why each is needed

PermissionReason
storage To save the items listed above on your own device. This permission grants access only to the extension's own storage, not to anything else on your computer.
https://*.facebook.com/* To read and modify the appearance of Facebook pages you open. The extension is not active on any other website.

Changes the extension can make to your Facebook account

This is not data collection, but it is worth stating plainly. Two optional features, both switched off unless you turn them on, use Facebook's own “Hide all from…” setting on your behalf:

  • a “Never show from this” button on posts, which acts only when you click it;
  • an automatic sweep, which applies the same setting to unchosen sources as they appear.

These change a preference on your Facebook account. The effect is permanent until you reverse it on Facebook (Settings → News Feed → Reconnect), and it applies wherever you use Facebook, including on your phone. Every use is recorded in the local log described above so you can review what was done.

Your data, and deleting it

Because nothing is ever sent anywhere, there is no copy of your data for anyone to disclose, correct or delete on your behalf. You hold the only copy, and you can remove it at any time:

  • Clear index and Clear stats in the extension's popup remove the stored friends, groups and counts.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it has stored, permanently.

Note that removing the extension does not undo any “Hide all from…” settings already applied to your Facebook account; those are reversed on Facebook itself.

Third parties

The extension bundles no third-party code, libraries or services. It loads nothing from the internet at runtime. No third party receives any information from it, because it sends none.

Your use of Facebook remains governed by Facebook's own terms and privacy policy. This extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Meta Platforms, Inc.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's handling of data ever changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Because the extension collects nothing, any change here would represent a significant change in what it does.
